# Skill totals — v0.98

Open the bank once per session. The summary scans bank slots, calculates incrementally on Logic and caches totals for skill hovers. All ten supported skills use native tooltip text and frame components. Banked XP follows Bonus XP when present, otherwise Remainder.

For Archaeology, Banked XP is the greater of the material-limited restoration estimate and the potential restoration XP of owned, level-eligible damaged artefacts. Potential assumes the missing restoration materials will be obtained. When potential exceeds the ready estimate, a separate Ready XP row reports restoration XP supported by the scanned bank supplies. Material storage is not scanned. The audit log records both material-limited XP and artefact-potential. Repaired artefacts do not earn restoration XP again. Potential does not depend on the setting that filters menu recipes with missing ingredients.

Other skills retain their supply-limited estimates. Highest modeled usable routes are preferred by XP per primary ingredient and then recipe XP. This is a deterministic greedy estimate, not a proven global optimum. Shared supplies are consumed once within each skill. Skill totals are alternative uses of the bank; do not add different skill totals together as a combined training plan. Future completed-product chains and unscanned containers are excluded. Enabled outfit and deterministic equipment bonuses still apply. Stored bonus XP is displayed by the game and is not applied to these estimates.

All 26 local suites pass. Native client verification is still required.
